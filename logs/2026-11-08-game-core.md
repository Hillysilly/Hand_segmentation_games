## What was built

**Core rules** (`Assets/Scripts/Core/`, plain C#, unit-tested):

- `Fighter`:
  - Starts at 100 HP. Max HP is 150 at level 2 and 200 at level 3.
  - Healing: after 5 s without dealing or taking damage, 3 HP a second.
  - The meter fills only from damage you deal; 60 damage fills it.
  - Transforming heals half the new max HP.
  - Dying costs a life and drops you back to level 1 with an empty meter. You respawn after 5 s with 2 s of protection.
  - Powerups: invincible for 8 s, 1.5x damage for 10 s, +30% meter.
- `ShieldGauge`: 3 s of holding, then it breaks for 6 s. It recharges at 0.5 s per second, and needs 0.3 s in the gauge to go up again.
- `WaveDirector`: a 3 s countdown, then waves of 2 + 2×wave enemies. At most 4 alive at once, 1.6 s apart, with 6 s between waves.
- `RoundTracker`: best of 3, for PVP.
- `CharacterDef`: the 6 characters, with placeholder names, colours, aura styles per level and controls text.

**Gesture library** (`Assets/Scripts/Gestures/`, unit-tested with made-up hands):

- Hand shapes: fist, open, OK, finger gun (index and middle out, thumb up), spider sign, cup.
- Detectors:
  - shield X and power-up pose
  - pause: OK with both hands, held 2 s
  - two-hand charge then push, and triangle then fists
  - bow: fists together, draw, open
  - finger-gun flick, which fires along the aim from before the flick, blended with the arm line
  - punch: a fist faster than 2.5 m/s
  - web sign, cup grab/throw, saber grip, underhand throw
- Shoulders are estimated from the head, since hand tracking can't see elbows.

**Game on the headset** (`Assets/Scripts/Game/`, `Assets/Scripts/UI/`):

- `Arena` runs the flow: title, mode, character select (with colour), match, results. It also has the pause menu (double OK) and the debug menu.
- `RepulsorGame` became the weapon layer.
  - Its old orb game is behind **Debug > Old controls**.
  - Hits now go through `Hittables`, with HP. `Damage` holds the values: repulsor 5, laser 10/s (+40% per level), shockwave 6.
- Orbs with 20 HP stand in for enemies until robots exist.
- Technoman:
  - Level 1 has the laser.
  - Level 2 adds repulsors. After a repulsor shot, the laser waits for a fresh open palm.
  - Others use blasts until their own attacks are built.
  - The laser no longer fires during an OK sign.
- **Poke buttons:**
  - Keys sink into a dark hollow, click 9 mm down and spring back.
  - A ring brightens as your finger nears.
  - Your finger must arrive from the front, so a panel opening over your hand can't click itself.
- **HUD:**
  - It sits low right, drifting after your head.
  - It shows a fighting-game HP bar with a red trail, the meter (it pulses when ready, with a "fists at your sides" hint), lives, the shield gauge, powerup timers and an optional FPS readout.
  - Big messages go in the middle: countdown, wave, knocked out.
- Overhead bars on enemies show only within 25° of your gaze and once the enemy is hurt, or for 2 s after a hit.
- **Shield bubble:** one `Bubble.shader` with 5 styles: hex, flame, glass, thin, web. It's faint from inside (your own) and full strength from outside.
- The transformation burst is an expanding energy sphere. The knocked-out view is a grey dim.
- **Debug menu:**
  - Old controls, a gesture readout and an FPS toggle.
  - Spawn orb, hit me, knock me out, fill meter, god mode, skip wave, the three powerups, next aura.
  - **Variant Lab:** shield styles A-E in front of you, saved per character.
- **Gesture readout:** a panel left of your view.
  - It shows each hand's finger values, which shapes are on, and every detector as it fires, with aim lines.
  - It logs `[Gesture]` lines every second. This is the tool for today's tuning session.

**Editor:**

- `GameAssets.Build` makes the `Resources` materials and meshes.
- `GamePreview.RenderAll` renders the menus, HUD and shields to `unity_game/Logs/preview_*.png`.

## Room objects and co-op (pulled forward from 11/11-11/12)

- **Room objects** (`RoomObjects.cs`):
  - Space Setup bounding boxes are on (`ARBoundingBoxFeature`). An `ARBoundingBoxManager` is added once the scene permission is granted.
  - A cupped hand (Mage level 2, Saber level 3) or a web sign (Spider level 2) aimed at a box highlights it and lifts a glowing copy of it.
  - A fast swing throws the copy. It breaks on an enemy, the floor or a wall, and damages things nearby (10-40 by size). The real object can be used again after 8 s.
  - Untested on the headset, and it needs Space Setup done in the room.
- **Co-op** (`Assets/Scripts/Net/`, `Arena.Net.cs`):
  - **Connection:**
    - Plain UDP on port 47777.
    - The room code is the host's last address byte plus a check digit, so it needs no broadcast permission. It assumes both headsets are on the same /24 home network.
    - The internet permission is on (`ForceInternetPermission`).
  - **Lining up:** both players touch the same two real points (`SharedFrame`, `StillPointDetector`).
  - **What each player sees of the other:** health bar, level and shield, plus head and palm markers that show calibration error. Their blasts are drawn too.
  - **Enemies:**
    - The host runs enemies and waves and sends them 10 times a second.
    - The client draws copies its attacks can hit; hits go to the host, which owns HP.
    - Enemy bolts are fired at both players, and enemies chase the nearest one.
  - The match ends when both players are out of lives.
  - Tests:
    - A loopback test connects a host and a client for real.
    - Protocol and calibration-maths tests pass, 130 of 130 in total.
- **Not done:** the other player's aura (`AuraView` uses global shader values, so only one aura can show at a time), PVP and shared spatial anchors.

## PVP, feedback, tuning

- **PVP** (`Arena.Pvp.cs`):
  - The other player is a target your attacks can hit, and the hit is sent to them. Their headset applies it, so shields and invincibility count.
  - The host decides rounds: a knockout ends the round, best of 3, and levels reset each round.
  - With friendly fire on, co-op uses the same path.
- **Hurt flash:** taking damage flashes your view edges red and plays a zap.
- **Powerups you can see:** while 1.5x damage is on, everything you fire turns red. The invincibility powerup turns it gold. The other player sees a glow around you.
- **Live gesture tuning** (development builds):
  - On start the game writes every threshold to `files/tuning_current.txt`.
  - Push a `files/tuning.txt` with lines like `HandPose.ThumbOutAbove = 0.55`; it applies within 2 s, without a rebuild.
  - Path: `/sdcard/Android/data/com.alberthan.repulsortest/files/`.
- **Sounds:** the Kenney CC0 sci-fi pack (kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds) can't be downloaded by script, so it needs a browser. Synthesized sounds remain for now.
